About me

Hi! I’m Maylo

A Software Developer currently studying Creative Media & Game Technologies (CMGT) at Saxion University of Applied Sciences in Enschede.

After graduating as a Software Developer, I decided to continue my education and focus on what I’m most passionate about: game development. CMGT allows me to combine my technical background with my creative interests and develop my skills in creating games and interactive experiences.

My main role within game development is Programming. I enjoy building gameplay systems, mechanics, tools, and solving technical challenges. My background as a Software Developer gives me a strong foundation in programming and problem-solving, which I continue to expand through game development.

Alongside programming, my secondary role is Art. I enjoy exploring the visual side of game development through 3D, environments, and other creative work. I mainly work with Unity and C#, while also using tools such as Maya and ZBrush to support the artistic side of my projects.

What I enjoy most about game development is bringing different disciplines together. Whether I’m programming a gameplay mechanic or creating something for the visual side of a project, I like experimenting, solving problems, and turning ideas into something players can actually experience.

I’m constantly learning and improving my skills, with Programming as my main focus and Art as a supporting skill. My goal is to grow as a game developer and eventually contribute to games that are both technically strong and creatively engaging.
